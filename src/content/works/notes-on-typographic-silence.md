Negative space is not empty space. It is the pause between notes that gives the melody its meaning. In interface design, what you omit defines what remains.

> "Space is not a void to be filled, but a medium to be sculpted."

This project is a typographic study—a single-page experience where whitespace carries as much weight as the letterforms themselves. Every margin, every line-height, every absence was deliberate.

The result is a reading experience that breathes. No distractions. No decoration. Just type and silence.
