A blog assumes finished thoughts. A garden assumes living ones. The difference is not cosmetic—it changes how you write, how you link, and how you revisit old work.

> "Publish to think, not think to publish."

Digital gardening rewards connection over chronology. Notes link to notes. Ideas cross-pollinate. The site becomes a map of your mind, not a stack of posts.

Start with one seed. Water it. Let it grow toward the light of your curiosity.
